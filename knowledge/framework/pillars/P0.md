VT-8 CLASSIFICATION NOTE | If the vault's primary yield instrument is a publicly traded security (listed equity, listed preferred stock, listed bond, ETF, MMF), classify as VT-8. Do not classify as VT-3 (tokenised RWA) or VT-4 (structured finance). 

VT-3 vs VT-8 distinction: VT-3 primary risk = private credit or private fund. VT-8 primary risk = publicly traded instrument with SEC filings, daily listed liquidity, corporate issuer risk, and TradFi settlement chain.
