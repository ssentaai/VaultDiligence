# P18 · Party Standing

P18  ·  Party Standing  ·  Family E: Operational, Infrastructure & Governance
Vault-type applicability note: Applies to any vault that has at least one yield-critical party — a party fundamental to generating or protecting the yield (delegated strategy/investment manager, sub-advisor, real-asset custodian, or credit-critical counterparty). N/A for a vault with no delegated yield party (e.g. a fully immutable protocol vault whose yield is generated by autonomous contract logic with no external party) — state why; the absence of any delegated yield party is itself a finding worth recording.
Provenance: tree-native pillar, no xlsx provenance. P18 extends beyond the xlsx-migrated P0-P10 (like P12/P13 and P14-P17). Built 2026-07-08 from the party-standing reconciliation (an internal analysis), which found that the framework identified parties (F-ROL / P17) and pointed to token ratings (F-RAT) but never assessed a yield-critical party's own independent standing. Grounded by the the CLO fund manager event: S&P cut the CLO fund manager parent — sub-advisor to a tokenized CLO fund's CLO portfolio — from BBB+ (investment grade, affirmed March 2025) to BB (sub-investment grade) on completion of a buyout/merger into an acquirer on or about 30 June 2026, reflected in no a tokenized CLO fund fund document. Fields F-STD-001 through F-STD-007.
Scope: this pillar assesses PARTIES drawn from the F-ROL role registry (P17), not the vault or its token. It is distinct from F-RAT, which points to a rating of the token/fund, and from P14-P16, which assess the Risk Control Operator's control apparatus rather than any party's own standing. Standing is assessed ONLY for parties flagged yield-critical in F-STD-001; administrative parties (transfer agent, paying agent, fund administrator, legal counsel) are identified-only via F-ROL and receive no standing assessment here.
Principle: a yield-critical party's standing is a moving target. Every standing fact recorded in this pillar carries an as-of-date, and change-since-onboarding — a credit downgrade, a merger or acquisition, a change of control, a regulatory action, or a key-personnel departure — is a first-class field (F-STD-007), not a footnote. The rationale is direct: a yield-critical party's corporate standing can change after the vault's offering documents were written, and that change is frequently the dominant risk to the yield. Every rating, registration, filing, and change is recorded as third-party evidence with its source and date; nothing in this pillar is a VaultDiligence-assigned standing of a party — the allocator decides what the evidence means.

18.1 | Yield-Critical Designation | Yield-Critical Party Flag (F-STD-001) | Identify which named parties in the vault's stack (by F-ROL ID) are fundamental to generating or protecting the yield: the delegated strategy/investment manager (F-ROL-003), sub-advisor (F-ROL-004), real-asset custodian (F-ROL-007), and any credit-critical counterparty. State the reason each is yield-critical, with an as-of-date. This is a distinct judgment from F-FIN-083, which flags counterparties by exposure size and functioning-dependency; derive the yield-critical set independently and cross-reference F-FIN-083 for the exposure view rather than re-labelling it. This flag gates F-STD-002 through F-STD-007. | Gap with action | The set of yield-critical parties is stated by F-ROL ID with the reason each is fundamental to the yield and an as-of-date, derived independently of the exposure-materiality register (F-FIN-083). | An unflagged dominant yield party is a critical condition — a party on which the yield depends but which is not identified and flagged cannot have its standing assessed. | a tokenized CLO fund: the CLO yield is generated by sub-advisor the CLO fund manager, not the issuer — until that party is flagged yield-critical, its standing is invisible to the assessment.
18.2 | Credit Standing | Party Credit Rating (F-STD-002) | For each yield-critical party (or its ratable parent per F-STD-003), record the current independent corporate credit rating with the issuing agency (S&P / Moody's / Fitch / KBRA) named and the rating's as-of-date. Distinct from F-RAT, which points to a rating of the token/fund. A crypto-native or private party with no corporate credit rating has that absence recorded as the finding — not inferred, and not substituted with a token rating. | Gap with action | The party's current corporate credit rating is recorded from the rating agency with the agency named and the rating dated, or its absence is stated explicitly; the token rating (F-RAT) is never substituted for the party's own. | An undisclosed or unobtainable rating for a party whose creditworthiness is fundamental to the yield is a gap stated with a precise action, not an assumption of soundness. | the CLO fund manager carried an S&P corporate rating (BBB+, later BB) entirely separate from a tokenized CLO fund's token rating — the party rating is where the June 2026 downgrade appeared.
18.3 | Entity Structure | Ratable-Parent Resolution (F-STD-003) | Resolve each yield-critical party's named operating entity to its ultimate ratable parent (operating subsidiary -> rated group parent), recording the entity chain and an as-of-date, because the named operating entity is often thin while the parent is what is rated and what backs it. Verify via GLEIF LEI direct/ultimate parent relationships (gleif-lei source). | Gap with action | The operating-entity-to-ultimate-parent chain is resolved and dated from the entity registry, so credit rating and financial standing are read against the entity that actually carries them. | Where an operating subsidiary is unrated and its parent relationship is undisclosed, the party's true credit backing cannot be located — state the gap. | the CLO fund investment manager (operating entity) resolves to the CLO fund manager parent (the rated parent) — the rating lives at the parent, not the named subsidiary.
18.4 | Regulatory Standing | Party Regulatory Standing — Current (F-STD-004) | For each yield-critical party, record current regulatory registration/authorization and good standing (SEC / FCA / equivalent) with an as-of-date. Cross-reference F-ENT-021 (investment-manager regulatory status), F-ENT-041 (custodian regulatory status), and F-CUS-004 (prime-broker regulatory standing), which capture registration as static onboarding snapshots — this criterion adds the current, dated standing for the yield-critical party specifically. | Gap with action | Current registration and good standing for the yield-critical party is recorded and dated from the regulator's public register, not carried forward from an onboarding-time snapshot. | Loss, lapse, or restriction of a yield-critical party's authorization is a critical condition once identified; an unverified current standing is a gap with a precise action. | A regulated manager whose authorization is later restricted keeps its onboarding-time snapshot unless the current standing is re-checked and dated.
18.5 | Financial Strength | Party Financial Strength (F-STD-005) | For each yield-critical party, record financial-strength evidence from public filings — revenue, capital, solvency indicators, assets under management, insurance / E&O cover — with each figure sourced and given an as-of-date. Cross-reference F-ENT-023 (manager AUM) and F-CUS-005 (prime-broker insurance), which hold fragments; this criterion records the party's financial standing as a dated whole. | Gap with action | The party's financial strength is recorded from its public filings with each figure sourced and dated; fragments held elsewhere are cross-referenced, not duplicated. | A yield-critical party that publishes no financial evidence and holds no rating is a party whose solvency cannot be assessed — state the gap with the specific filing required. | A thinly-capitalised operating manager can carry a large mandate; without a dated financial read the deterioration is invisible until it fails.
18.6 | Track Record | Delegated-Manager Track Record (F-STD-006) | For the party managing the yield strategy, record strategy-specific tenure and history: how long it has managed this strategy, and its performance and AUM history in it, with an as-of-date. Distinct from F-ENT-024 (entity age) and F-ENT-025 (generic stress-event experience), which are proxies scoped to a different party or to generic stress rather than the delegated strategy. | Gap with action | The delegated manager's tenure and performance on this strategy are recorded and dated from strategy documentation or performance disclosure, not proxied by corporate age or generic experience. | A delegated manager with no disclosed track record on the specific strategy is a gap; the entity-age proxy (F-ENT-024) is not a substitute. | A firm decades old may be new to a given credit strategy — entity age (F-ENT-024) hides that; strategy tenure surfaces it.
18.7 | Recency & Change | Change Since Onboarding (F-STD-007) | Record any change in a yield-critical party's standing since the vault onboarded it — credit downgrade, merger or acquisition, change of control, regulatory action, or key-personnel departure — as a dated change-event with its source and the as-of-date of the check. An unchanged party records "no change since onboarding, as of [date]": silence is not a finding, but the check must be run and dated. This is the field the the CLO fund manager downgrade-plus-merger event would populate. | Gap with action | A dated change-check has been run against each yield-critical party since onboarding, recording any downgrade / M&A / control change / regulatory action / key-departure with its source, or an explicit dated "no change" where none is found. | A material change to a yield-critical party that the vault's documents do not reflect is a critical condition once identified; a change-check never run is a gap, not a clean result. | the CLO fund manager: BBB+ -> BB plus a change of control into an acquirer (June 2026), reflected in no a tokenized CLO fund fund document — the change, not the onboarding-time standing, was the dominant risk.
