# P2 · Custody

P2  ·  Custody & Counterparty Safety  ·  Family A: Capital Safety
Vault-type applicability note: Applies universally. Rehypothecation is a critical condition (RF01).

2.9 | Curator Stress Record | Curator Stress Event Response Record — Speed, Automation, Outcome | Has the curator been tested by a live collateral stress event? Benchmark: Steakhouse 2026-03-22 -- automated monitoring flagged 1.61% USR/USDC deviation at 02:21 UTC, fully exited by 03:02 UTC (41 minutes), $235,589 peak exposure, zero losses. A curator with no live stress event history cannot be benchmarked. | Gap with action | At least one documented stress event response. Automated monitoring confirmed. Response time <2 hours. Zero depositor losses in documented events. | RF32 | Steakhouse: 41min, automated, zero losses. Gauntlet/re7/kpk/9summits: Public Allocator auto-supplied millions into broken markets for hours post-exploit. No automated halt. Material depositor losses.

2.11 | TradFi Prime Brokerage | Prime Brokerage Custody Assessment — For Vaults Holding Listed Securities | For vaults where the underlying asset is a listed equity, bond, ETF, or other TradFi instrument held at a prime broker: standard crypto custody criteria (SOC-2, MPC, OES, crypto insurance) do not apply. Apply the following TradFi-specific assessment: (1) Is the prime broker a SIPC member? SIPC covers up to $500K per account for securities (not crypto). Confirm SIPC membership. (2) What are the prime brokerage agreement's margin lending and rehypothecation rights? Prime brokers have legal right to rehypothecate client securities in standard PB agreements unless explicitly prohibited. Confirm: is rehypothecation explicitly prohibited in the agreement? (3) What is the prime broker's own financial health? Capital adequacy ratio, recent audited financials, regulatory standing. (4) Execution and custody separation: are execution and custody functions held by the same entity or separated? WORKED PRECEDENT: the execution broker acts as execution broker. the prime broker acts as custodian. Separation is present. But the prime broker is a relatively new prime broker (founded 2018) with limited stress-event track record. (5) What standard TradFi insurance covers the position? SIPC, excess SIPC, fidelity bond? (6) What happens in prime broker insolvency? Under US law, client securities are segregated but the insolvency process takes months. Sources: prime brokerage agreement, broker SIPC confirmation, broker audited financials, FINRA BrokerCheck. | Gap with action. N/A for vaults holding only onchain assets — apply only where underlying is held at a TradFi prime broker. | SIPC member confirmed. Rehypothecation explicitly prohibited. Execution/custody separated. Prime broker financial health verified. Standard TradFi insurance coverage confirmed and quantified. | Prime broker not SIPC member; rehypothecation rights present in PB agreement; execution and custody in same entity; prime broker financial health unverified | MF Global 2011: client securities rehypothecated, $1.6B shortfall in client accounts. Lehman Brothers 2008: prime brokerage clients waited months for asset recovery despite technical segregation. TradFi prime brokerage risk is different from crypto custody risk — both need separate assessment.
