# P4 · Collateral

P4  ·  Collateral Quality & Liquidation Mechanism  ·  Family B: Strategy Integrity
Vault-type applicability note: Applies where Exposure != crypto-native OR venue = tradfi-primary (off-chain-held reserves); N/A for fully on-chain vaults with transparent accounting. [Phase 3 (2026-07-09): dimension gate; faithful re-expression of the former "Critical for VT-1, VT-4" scoping.]

4.1 | Asset Tier | Collateral Asset Quality & Native vs Wrapped | What assets back the vault? Native (BTC, ETH) vs wrapped vs synthetic vs algorithmic? Are bridge risks in the collateral chain quantified? | Gap with action | ≥80% Tier-1 native assets (BTC, ETH, regulated stablecoins). Zero algorithmic collateral. Wrapped tokens <20% with bridge audit required. | RF12 · Algorithmic/synthetic collateral >30%; wrapped >60% without bridge audit; collateral quality undisclosed | Terra/Luna: $40B collapsed because collateral was algorithmic. UST backed by LUNA = circular, not real collateral.
4.2 | Over-Collateralisation | Collateralisation Ratio, Buffer & Dynamic Adjustment | What is the current CR? What is the minimum before liquidation? Is CR dynamic (adjusts to volatility) or static? What is the liquidation buffer? | Gap with action | CR ≥150% for volatile assets; ≥110% for regulated stablecoins. Dynamic CR that tightens in stress. Liquidation buffer quantified and published. | CR <120% for volatile assets; static CR; no dynamic adjustment; buffer not disclosed | Black Thursday (Maker): CR was adequate in normal markets but cascade liquidations in 12-hour period overwhelmed the system
4.3 | Liquidation Mechanism | Dutch Auction + Stability Pool Architecture | How does liquidation work? Fixed price or Dutch auction? Is there a stability pool for instant liquidation? What happens if no liquidators show up? | Gap with action | Dutch auction (gradual price discovery). Stability pool as backstop capital. Cascade circuit breakers. Liquidation discount ≥8%. Tested under stress. | RF19 · Fixed-price liquidation for volatile collateral; no backstop; cascade modelling absent; liquidators not incentivised; untested in stress | Maker Black Thursday: fixed-price auction with no bidders meant DAI was minted for $0. $8M bad debt. Dutch auctions implemented after.
4.4 | Proof of Reserves | Real-Time Proof of Reserves & On-Chain Verification | Can depositors verify that the stated collateral exists on-chain? How often is PoR published? Is it cryptographic or self-reported? | Gap with action | Real-time on-chain PoR. Cryptographic attestation. Dune/Nansen dashboard. <5min data lag. Third-party attestation monthly minimum. | RF04 · No PoR; monthly self-reported only; custodian refuses verification; discrepancy between reported and on-chain | FTX: no independent PoR. Alameda balance sheet had $8B 'FTT token' as collateral against customer funds — all self-reported.
4.5 | Concentration Risk | Collateral Concentration & Correlation Analysis | Is one asset type >50% of collateral? Has correlation between collateral assets been modelled in a tail event (when correlations spike to 1)? | Gap with action | No single collateral type >50% TVL. Correlation matrix published. Stress scenario assumes all assets move together in tail event. | Single asset >70%; no concentration policy; tail correlation not modelled; diversification benefit assumed to persist in crisis | SBF portfolio: appeared diversified but all assets were correlated crypto. In May 2022 crash, every asset fell simultaneously.
4.6 | Liquidation Liquidity | On-Chain Liquidation Capacity at Scale | Can liquidators execute at scale? DEX depth for each collateral asset at 5x, 10x position size? Slippage modelled? | Gap with action | Each collateral type liquidatable at ≥$10M in single block with <2% slippage. DEX depth stress-tested. Liquidators actively incentivised. | Collateral DEX depth <$1M; single-venue liquidation; slippage not modelled; no liquidator incentive programme | Multiple DeFi protocols: insufficient DEX liquidity for long-tail collateral meant liquidations caused additional price impact loops
4.11 | Governance Response | Timelock Duration vs Critical Stress Response Window | What is the governance timelock duration for parameter changes? What is the realistic stress response window for this specific collateral type (minutes for synthetic stablecoins; hours/days for RWAs)? Calculate timelock-to-response-window ratio. When timelock exceeds response window, curator intervention during stress is structurally impossible. Flag any ratio >1.0. | Gap with action | Timelock duration shorter than realistic stress window for this collateral type. Curator can intervene meaningfully. | RF34 | Resolv exploit unfolded in 17 minutes. Any vault with >1hr timelock had zero curator intervention capability. Steakhouse exited in 41min only via automated monitoring with no governance constraint.
4.7 | Loop Exposure — Real-Time | External Leverage Loop Size — Total Borrow Against Vault Token | Query Morpho, Euler, Fluid, Aave, and Compound for all positions using this vault token as collateral. Calculate: (1) total USD borrowed against vault token across all protocols, (2) estimated leverage multiplier = total borrow / vault TVL, (3) primary exit pool for correlated unwind and its current depth, (4) expected slippage at 20% / 50% simultaneous loop unwind. This is a real-time on-chain calculation, not a point-in-time snapshot. Flag if leverage multiplier exceeds 30% of TVL. | Gap with action | Leverage multiplier <20% TVL. Primary exit pool depth >3x estimated correlated unwind at 20% TVL. | RF35 | Resolv: looped wstUSR positions routed through single Curve USR/USDC pool. Correlated unwind crashed USR to $0.025 in 17 minutes.
4.8 | Loop Exposure — Contagion Map | Cross-Protocol Contagion Path — Named Protocols and Cascade Sequence | Map the exact cascade sequence if this vault token depegs or minting is compromised: (1) which lending protocols hold this token as collateral, (2) which vaults have exposure via those lending protocols, (3) which DEX pools provide exit liquidity for all of the above simultaneously, (4) at what price impact does the cascade become self-reinforcing. Name every protocol in the chain. This is the Anastasiia failure mode 2 (recovery endogeneity) made concrete for this specific vault. | Gap with action | Full contagion map documented. No single exit pool concentration >50% of correlated unwind liquidity. | RF35 | Gauntlet/re7/kpk/9summits auto-allocation fed millions into broken Resolv markets via single Public Allocator mechanism. Contagion was entirely mappable in advance.
4.12 | Loss Waterfall | Depositor Position in Loss Waterfall — Senior vs Junior | What is the depositor's position in the loss absorption hierarchy? (1) Identify all tranches: senior, mezzanine, junior, equity. (2) Name the first-loss mechanism and who bears it. (3) What failure modes does the first-loss tranche protect against? (4) What failure modes fall OUTSIDE the first-loss protection scope? Steakhouse precedent: Steakhouse explicitly did not underwrite RLP for depositors because RLP carries concentrated tail risk from collateral pool events. Jesus Rodriguez precedent: RLP first-loss mandate designed for funding rate losses and liquidation costs -- NOT supply-side inflation attacks. The threat model scope of the first-loss tranche is the critical question. | Gap with action | Depositor position clearly senior to at least one first-loss layer. First-loss threat model scope explicitly documented. Supply-side, oracle, and operational failure modes covered. | RF39 | USR holders: senior. RLP: junior, designed for collateral-side losses only. Supply-side mint attack bypassed RLP protection entirely. Senior depositors lost value despite first-loss structure.
