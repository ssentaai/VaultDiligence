9.4 | Redemption Stress | Redemption Run Capacity — 10%, 25%, 50% TVL | Can protocol process 10%, 25%, 50% TVL redemption in 48 hours without breaking the peg or triggering collateral cascade? Liquidity buffer pre-positioned?

FULL-INFORMATION RUN ASSESSMENT (Anastasiia framework): In DeFi, all state is public. Utilisation rate, withdrawal queue, vault composition are observable at block level. The coordination problem that makes TradFi bank runs probabilistic becomes near-deterministic in DeFi. Assess: at what combination of observable conditions (utilisation rate, health factor, collateral price deviation) does rational depositor behaviour shift to immediate withdrawal? Nash equilibrium threshold: when depositors observe utilisation approaching 100% AND collateral deteriorating simultaneously, first-mover advantage is total. Those who exit first receive full value. Those who exit last bear the loss. This threshold is calculable per vault and should be stated explicitly. | Gap with action | 30%+ TVL redemption capacity modelled and tested. Pre-positioned liquidity buffers. No cascade to collateral liquidation. 10%/25%/50% scenarios published. | No redemption run modelling; liquidity buffer <5% TVL; 30% redemption triggers cascade; no published scenario analysis | USDC depeg (SVB crisis): $3.3B redemption in 24 hours stressed the system but Circle's direct treasury backing ultimately held

9.9 | Run Dynamics | Full-Information Run Nash Equilibrium — Observable Trigger Conditions | At what combination of publicly observable on-chain conditions does the Nash equilibrium for a rational depositor shift to immediate withdrawal? (1) Calculate the utilisation rate threshold above which first-mover advantage becomes total. (2) Identify the collateral health factor or price deviation that, combined with high utilisation, makes withdrawal the dominant strategy for all depositors simultaneously. (3) Estimate the time window between trigger condition becoming observable and meaningful exit capacity remaining. Anastasiia framework: DeFi runs are faster, sharper, and more complete than TradFi equivalents because information asymmetry -- the friction that makes TradFi runs probabilistic -- does not exist on-chain. This threshold should be disclosed to depositors as the de facto circuit breaker condition. | Gap with action | Nash equilibrium trigger conditions calculated and disclosed. Time window from trigger to exit capacity exhaustion estimated. Depositors informed of observable conditions that signal immediate exit. | | Morpho/Euler/Fluid during Resolv exploit: utilisation hit 100% within minutes.
