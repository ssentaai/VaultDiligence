# SC13 — L1 reorganisation deeper than the stated finality assumption

## Scenario
A chain carrying vault exposure experiences a reorganisation deeper than the finality assumption the vault's accounting relies on. Transactions believed final are rewritten: a redemption, liquidation, or deposit that settled is reversed, double-spend windows open, and any process that treated N-block confirmation as final is retroactively wrong.

## Trigger conditions (observable)
- A reorg depth exceeds the vault's (or its oracle's, or its bridge's) assumed confirmation count.
- The chain conflates probabilistic, economic, and deterministic finality in its documentation, so the true safe-confirmation depth was never bounded.
- A bridge or oracle on the deployment finalises on a confirmation count shallower than the observed reorg depth.

## What to assess (evidence only)
1. Is the finality model documented with probabilistic / economic / deterministic stated separately, and is finality lag bounded? (F-CHN-004)
2. What is the chain's reorg history, including any reorg deeper than the standard finality assumption? (F-CHN-006)
3. What confirmation depth do the vault's bridge (F-BRG-001) and oracle (F-ORC-013) treat as final, and does it exceed the worst observed reorg?
4. Does the chain bound the vault's exposure tier given its finality record? (F-CHN-010)

## Documented response expected
A response stating the assumed-final confirmation depth across the vault's settlement, bridge, and oracle paths, benchmarked against the chain's worst observed reorg, and what happens to a redemption caught in a reorg. If none exists: state the gap with a precise action.

## Precedent
Polygon, 2022: a reorganisation deeper than common finality assumptions reorganised a large run of blocks; positions assuming finality at a shallower depth were retroactively rewritten.
