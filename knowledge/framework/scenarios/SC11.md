# SC11 — Sequencer outage on an L2 deployment

## Scenario
The sequencer of an L2 carrying vault exposure halts or censors transaction inclusion. Deposits, withdrawals, liquidations, oracle updates, and redemptions on that deployment freeze for the duration. Depositors cannot exit and liquidators cannot act, regardless of how clean the vault's own contracts or collateral are; the vault inherits the chain's liveness failure.

## Trigger conditions (observable)
- A single-sequencer L2 stops producing blocks, or selectively censors inclusion.
- Forced-inclusion / escape-hatch latency (if any) exceeds the vault's stress response window.
- Oracle updates on the L2 stall, so on-chain prices go stale during the outage.

## What to assess (evidence only)
1. Is the sequencer single, shared, or permissionless, and is there a documented path to permissionless sequencing? (F-CHN-003)
2. Is there a forced-inclusion path or escape hatch, and what is its standing latency? Absence is a single point of failure on the sequencer. (F-CHN-004)
3. What is the chain's halt history — frequency, duration, recovery time, published post-mortems? (F-CHN-006)
4. During an outage, do the vault's oracles revert on staleness or serve a stale price? (F-ORC-013, P3.3)
5. Does the chain bound the vault's exposure tier (F-CHN-010), and is that bound stated in the pack?

## Documented response expected
A response that names: the escape-hatch mechanism and its latency, what happens to in-flight redemptions and liquidations during an outage, and whether the vault's exposure on this chain is sized to the chain's liveness record. If none exists: state the gap with a precise action (request the issuer's L2-outage runbook).

## Precedent
Arbitrum sequencer outages (December 2021 and June 2022) halted chain-wide transaction inclusion for the duration — every position on the chain was frozen regardless of asset quality. This is the inherited-liveness failure the field set above exists to surface.
