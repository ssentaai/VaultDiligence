# RF47

RF47 | MATERIAL | P10 | 10.1 DAO Liability Classification | Curator or protocol operates as an unregistered DAO with no legal wrapper, exposing governance-token holders, voters, and active participants to general-partnership joint-and-several personal liability. Precedent: Ooki (N.D.Cal. 2023) and Sarcuni (S.D.Cal. 2023) treated unwrapped DAOs as general partnerships. | F-LEG-025, F-PRJ-002 | F-LEG-025 entity classified as unregistered DAO AND no liability-limiting wrapper (DAO LLC / foundation) present = TRIGGER | DAO adopts a liability-limiting wrapper (Wyoming or Marshall Islands DAO LLC, Cayman foundation, or equivalent) and discloses it. Until then the personal-liability exposure to governance participants stands as a surfaced condition for the allocator to weigh. | ALL
