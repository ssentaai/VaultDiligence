# RF45

RF45 | BLOCKING | P13 | 13.3/13.4 Chain Critical Property | A chain carrying material vault exposure has a critical property below baseline: a single sequencer with no forced-inclusion path or escape hatch, validator or upgrade control below honest-majority concentrated in one entity or jurisdiction, a sub-day timelock on production-critical chain upgrades, or probabilistic finality without a bounded reorg depth. Surfaced as a critical condition; the allocator decides. | F-CHN-003, F-CHN-004, F-CHN-005 | F-CHN-003 single sequencer with no escape hatch OR F-CHN-004 no bounded reorg depth / no forced-inclusion path OR F-CHN-005 sub-day upgrade timelock or sub-honest-majority upgrade control, on a deployment with material exposure = TRIGGER | Chain remediates the property (permissionless sequencing or a forced-inclusion escape hatch, honest-majority upgrade control, observation-window timelock on production upgrades) or vault exposure is sized to the chain bound per F-CHN-010. Until then the condition stands. | ALL
