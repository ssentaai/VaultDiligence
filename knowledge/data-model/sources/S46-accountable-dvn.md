# S46 — Accountable DVN

## What It Is

Privacy-preserving real-time financial verification platform.
Connects to wallets, custodians, and banks to verify financial data
directly at the source. Powers Proof of Solvency, Vault-as-a-Service,
and YieldApp.

Raised: $7.5M led by Pantera Capital. OKX Ventures participating.
Verified: $1B+ in assets across the execution broker, Amber Group, K3 Capital.

## What DVN Covers

Asset existence: confirmed holdings at verified custodians.
Liability balance: real-time balance sheet verification.
Proof of Solvency: continuous assets vs liabilities verification.

## What DVN Does NOT Cover

Redemption waterfall structure.
Legal isolation and bankruptcy remoteness.
Smart contract privileged roles or upgrade mechanisms.
Audit scope and gaps.
Counterparty map.
Nash equilibrium or run dynamics.
Oracle configuration.

## How to Cite in a Pack

  Date: [YYYY-MM-DD]. URL: accountable.capital/[vault].
  Covers: asset existence and liability balance only.

  smart contract architecture, or counterparty map."

Section: Credit and Collateral Risk (asset existence).
         Legal and Custody Risk (if proof of reserves cited).
         Source Appendix (always).

## What It Does Not Replace

A DVN proof does not replace any VaultDiligence investigation criterion.
It is corroborating evidence for the asset existence field only.
All other criteria require independent investigation.
