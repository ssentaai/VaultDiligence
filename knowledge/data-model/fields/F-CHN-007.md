# F-CHN-007

**Field ID**: F-CHN-007
**Category**: Chain
**Sub-Category**: Ecosystem
**Field Name**: Ecosystem Adoption & Market Infrastructure
**What to Collect / Question to Answer**: What is the chain's adoption as structural fact (chain TVL, stablecoin TVL and dominance, active addresses, protocol count and concentration), its DeFi vertical coverage with named gaps that affect vault operation, its DEX depth on major pairs, its bridge accessibility, on/off-ramp corridors, wallet support, competing lending markets, and its crisis-coordination capacity?
**Data Type**: Structured text (TVL, stablecoin TVL/dominance, active addresses, protocol count/concentration; DeFi vertical coverage + gaps; DEX depth; bridge list; ramp corridors; wallet support; competing markets; crisis-coordination entities)
**Vault Types**: ALL
**Collection Tier**: T1
**Pillar(s)**: P13
**Primary Source**: On-chain TVL and DEX-depth data (DeFiLlama chain page, DEX pool reserves on-chain); protocol and stablecoin dominance metrics; bridge and wallet support documentation
**Fallback Source**: Third-party ecosystem analytics (DeFiLlama, Dune dashboards, Token Terminal, L2BEAT activity metrics)
**Evidence Pathway**: Inspection-validatable: read major-pair DEX pool reserves on-chain to confirm liquidation-relevant depth; Third-party-evidenced: cite DeFiLlama / Dune for chain TVL, stablecoin dominance, active addresses, and protocol concentration, and name each bridge and crisis-coordination entity.
**Institutional Standard**: Ecosystem supports the primitives the vault depends on; liquidation-relevant DEX depth present; more than one bridge available; on/off-ramp corridors and wallet support documented; crisis-coordination capacity identified.
**Status**: Gap with action
**If Not Found — Gap Action**: Name the missing adoption metric, DeFi primitive, bridge, ramp corridor, or crisis-coordination entity and source it from on-chain data and analytics platforms; flag insufficient DEX depth for liquidation execution, single-bridge dependence, or missing critical oracle infrastructure as critical conditions.
**Source / Precedent**: a published protocol risk framework, 9 Jun 2026, §4.7: ecosystems with insufficient DEX depth for liquidation execution, single-bridge dependence, missing critical DeFi primitives, or absent crisis-response coordination capacity materially constrain the chain's exposure tier.
**Criterion ID(s)**: 13.7
