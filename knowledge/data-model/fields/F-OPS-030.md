# F-OPS-030 — Quantitative Risk Reporting

**Field ID**: F-OPS-030
**Field Name**: Quantitative Risk Reporting
**Pillar(s)**: P8
**Vault Types**: ALL
**Collection Tier**: T1
**Primary Source**: Protocol docs / Dashboard
**D1**: N
**D2**: Y
**Required?**: N
**TTL**: 720h (30 days)

## Description

Does the curator produce regular quantitative risk reports including VaR estimates, stress test scenarios, yield breakdown by source, and risk exposure by counterparty. Frequency and format.

## Failure Mode

An institutional allocator cannot defend a position without quantitative risk metrics. Absence of VaR or stress test reporting is a material governance gap for positions above $1M.

## If Not Found — Gap Action

Confirm whether curator produces quantitative risk reports: VaR estimates, stress test results, yield breakdown, risk exposure by counterparty. If no reports exist: G3 — curator does not produce quantitative risk reporting.
