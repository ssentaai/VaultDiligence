# F-FIN-079

**Field ID**: F-FIN-079
**Category**: Financial
**Sub-Category**: Reserve Reconciliation
**Field Name**: Reserve Reconciliation Authority and Segregation of Duties
**What to Collect / Question to Answer**: Who is responsible for reconciling on-chain reserves against reported balances, what tooling and independent oversight exists, and is the reconciling party segregated from the party that controls the reserves?
**Data Type**: Text (named role/entity + tooling + segregation statement)
**Vault Types**: ALL
**Collection Tier**: T3
**Pillar(s)**: P9
**Primary Source**: Operator DDQ response / reconciliation policy document / fund administrator engagement letter
**Fallback Source**: Operator website disclosure or interview, labelled not-confirmed-in-formal-documentation until a document is produced
**Evidence Pathway**: Third-party-evidenced: name the reconciling party and the oversight body (administrator, internal audit, board) and obtain the reconciliation policy or administrator engagement letter that defines the duty.
**Institutional Standard**: An independent party (not the reserve controller) reconciles on-chain reserves to reported balances on a stated cadence, with named tooling and an oversight body; reconciliation and reserve-control duties are held by separate parties so no single party can both move reserves and attest to them.
**Status**: Gap with action
**If Not Found — Gap Action**: Name the entity required to confirm reconciliation authority and request the reconciliation policy document and segregation-of-duties matrix; if the operator self-reconciles with no independent check, classify the segregation dimension as G2 with the specific document (administrator engagement letter or internal-audit charter) needed to close it.
**Source / Precedent**: Steakhouse Financial stablecoin-issuer DDQ v1.0, section 4.5: 'Who is responsible for reconciling reserves with reported balances, and what tooling or oversight exists?' Proof-of-reserves shows a number; reconciliation authority shows whether anyone independent verifies it.
**Criterion ID(s)**: 9.10
