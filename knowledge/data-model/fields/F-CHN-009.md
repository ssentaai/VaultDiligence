# F-CHN-009

**Field ID**: F-CHN-009
**Category**: Chain
**Sub-Category**: Base Liquidity
**Field Name**: Native & Major-Asset Liquidity
**What to Collect / Question to Answer**: What is the chain's structural liquidity backbone: the native token's DEX volume distribution, price stability and collateralisation rates; the availability and depth of major assets (ETH-correlated, BTC-correlated, stablecoins); and the decomposition of volume into organic versus incentive-driven flow?
**Data Type**: Structured text (native-token volume distribution, price stability, collateralisation; major-asset availability + depth by class; organic vs incentive-driven volume decomposition)
**Vault Types**: ALL
**Collection Tier**: T1
**Pillar(s)**: P13
**Primary Source**: On-chain DEX pool reserves and volume for the native token and major assets; incentive-programme disclosures tying volume to rewards; price-stability history from on-chain or oracle data
**Fallback Source**: Third-party liquidity analytics (DeFiLlama DEX volume, Dune liquidity dashboards) and named incentive-programme documentation
**Evidence Pathway**: Inspection-validatable: read the deepest native-token and major-asset DEX pool reserves on-chain and confirm LP-set diversity from pool holder distribution; Third-party-evidenced: cite DeFiLlama/Dune volume decomposition and the named incentive-programme documentation for the incentive-driven share.
**Institutional Standard**: Deep, stable, organically-supported liquidity in the chain's native and major assets, with diverse LP sets on the chain's deepest pools and volume not materially dependent on active incentive programmes.
**Status**: Gap with action
**If Not Found — Gap Action**: Name the missing measure (native-token volume distribution, price stability, collateralisation, major-asset depth, or organic-vs-incentive decomposition) and source it from on-chain pool data and incentive-programme disclosures; flag native-token instability, thin LP diversity on the chain's deepest pools, or volume materially supported by incentive programmes as critical conditions, since they propagate to every listing's exit depth.
**Source / Precedent**: a published protocol risk framework, 9 Jun 2026, §4.9: native-token instability, thin LP diversity on the chain's deepest pools, or volume materially supported by active incentive programmes constrains the standing exposure tier for every asset listed on the chain.
**Criterion ID(s)**: 13.9
