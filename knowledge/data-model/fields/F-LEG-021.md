# F-LEG-021

**Field ID**: F-LEG-021
**Category**: Legal
**Sub-Category**: Investor & Creditor Rights
**Field Name**: Investor/Creditor Rights + Competing-Claim Seniority
**What to Collect / Question to Answer**: What specific, enforceable rights do holders have (information and reporting rights, negative or financial covenants, veto rights, step-in rights, security interests), and are there any classes of stakeholders with superior or pari-passu claims over the same assets that would structurally subordinate the holder?
**Data Type**: Text
**Vault Types**: ALL
**Collection Tier**: T3
**Pillar(s)**: P1
**Primary Source**: Master agreement / subscription documents and the capital/claim structure — covenant package, security-interest grants, and the ranking of all claims over the same asset pool
**Fallback Source**: UCC / collateral-registry filings and on-chain collateral-lock contracts read to identify prior or pari-passu security interests over the same assets
**Evidence Pathway**: Inspection-validatable: read the collateral contract and any on-chain lien/escrow to confirm whether another claimant has a senior or equal position over the same assets; corroborate with the executed agreement's covenant and security-interest schedule.
**Institutional Standard**: Holders hold specific enforceable rights — information/reporting and negative covenants, with veto, step-in, or a perfected security interest where warranted — and the documentation discloses every class of stakeholder with a superior or pari-passu claim over the same assets, so the holder's true seniority is unambiguous.
**Status**: Gap with action
**If Not Found — Gap Action**: Request the full covenant and security-interest schedule and a complete claims-ranking table over the shared asset pool. The audit returned no field matches for step-in / covenant / pari-passu; absence means the holder may be silently subordinated — G2 (request) or G3 (rights do not exist and operator must create them).
**Source / Precedent**: Steakhouse §5.3 framework requirement: 'What specific, enforceable rights...? Are there any classes of stakeholders with superior or pari passu claims over the same assets?' The audit found step-in/covenant/pari-passu returned no field matches — whether the allocator has covenant leverage or is structurally subordinated was unsourced.
