# F-CUR-026

**Field ID**: F-CUR-026
**Category**: Curator
**Sub-Category**: Coverage Disclosure
**Field Name**: Requirement-Level Coverage Map Published
**What to Collect / Question to Answer**: Does the risk control operator publish a requirement-level map of its own risk framework against its monitoring implementation, marking each requirement full / partial / not-covered? Confirm the map exists and is requirement-level, not a single headline figure. Record its presence and the full / partial / none counts.
**Data Type**: Structured (map present Boolean; counts of full / partial / not-covered by requirement)
**Vault Types**: ALL (curator-managed; N/A for unmanaged/immutable vaults with no active Risk Control Operator — gate off F-ROL-001 Risk Control Operator, do not assume the curator fills it, state why)
**Collection Tier**: T2
**Pillar(s)**: P16
**Primary Source**: Risk Control Operator-published coverage map (framework requirement vs monitoring implementation)
**Fallback Source**: Risk Control Operator DDQ response; screenshot of a coverage-summary surface
**Evidence Pathway**: Third-party-evidenced: obtain the risk control operator's published coverage map and record the per-requirement full/partial/none status and counts; confirm it is requirement-level, not a single headline number. Record the counts as evidence, not a single summary figure.
**Institutional Standard**: The risk control operator publishes a requirement-by-requirement coverage map (full / partial / none) of its framework vs what it actually monitors; a risk control operator that can produce one is disclosing its own blind spots.
**Status**: Gap with action
**If Not Found — Gap Action**: Ask the risk control operator to produce its requirement-level coverage map. If it cannot, or discloses only a single headline figure with no per-requirement breakdown: G2 (request) or G3 (it does not maintain one) — its blind spots are unstated. Net-new (de-verdicted transparency question, no pass/fail gate).
**Source / Precedent**: Concept surfaced by the risk-infrastructure provider methodology (pp.3-4, requirement-to-monitoring coverage map); validated on first principles — the transparency of publishing a coverage map is the signal, not any particular coverage figure.
**Criterion ID(s)**: 16.1
