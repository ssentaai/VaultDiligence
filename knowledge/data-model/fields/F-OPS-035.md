# F-OPS-035

**Field ID**: F-OPS-035
**Category**: Operations
**Sub-Category**: Continuity
**Field Name**: Key-Person Redundancy + Tested Continuity
**What to Collect / Question to Answer**: If the lead operator is unavailable for 48 to 72 hours, who assumes their duties, and has that succession been tested or documented?
**Data Type**: Text (named successor + duties) + Boolean (tested/documented)
**Vault Types**: ALL
**Collection Tier**: T2
**Pillar(s)**: P8
**Primary Source**: Operator business-continuity / succession plan naming the backup for the lead operator's duties
**Fallback Source**: Operator DDQ response; multisig signer roster evidencing backup signers (cross-ref F-OPS-011); record of a continuity test
**Evidence Pathway**: Third-party-evidenced: obtain the documented succession plan naming who assumes the lead operator's duties on a 48-to-72-hour absence, and evidence that the handover has been tested or at minimum documented (drill record, runbook, or backup-signer roster).
**Institutional Standard**: Good practice is a named, documented backup who can assume the lead operator's duties within 48 to 72 hours with the handover tested at least once; a single point of failure on one person with no documented or tested successor is a continuity gate independent of contract risk.
**Status**: Gap with action
**If Not Found — Gap Action**: Ask the operator who assumes the lead operator's duties on a 48-to-72-hour absence and whether this has been tested or documented. If a successor is named but the handover is untested: E(P) — state untested. If no successor is named: G3 — operator must designate and document one. Cross-reference SC9 (continuity scenario) and F-OPS-011 (backup signers).
**Source / Precedent**: Steakhouse Financial stablecoin-issuer DDQ v1.0, section 6.5: 'If the lead operator is unavailable for 48–72 hours, who assumes their duties? Has this been tested or documented?' F-OPS-002 names personnel and SC9 mentions continuity only at scenario level; neither captures tested key-person redundancy. Drift April 2026 reinforces key-person/continuity exposure.
**Criterion ID(s)**: 8.2, SC9
