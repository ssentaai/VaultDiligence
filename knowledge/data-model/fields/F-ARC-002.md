# F-ARC-002

**Field ID**: F-ARC-002
**Category**: Archetype
**Sub-Category**: Fit & Classification Completeness
**Field Name**: Archetype Fit & Classification-Gap Acknowledgment
**What to Collect / Question to Answer**: AFTER committing a full best-effort classification on EVERY archetype dimension (per knowledge/framework/archetype/dimensions.md — the classifier MUST first assign a value or a multi-value set on Exposure, Strategy, Management, Structure, Seniority, Liquidity, and the venue flag; this field NEVER substitutes for that effort), record the RESIDUAL fit as a supplement. (1) FIT STATUS per dimension: clean-single-value (one value fits) / multi-value-set (composite — LIST the values) / no-clean-fit (novel on this axis — name the closest value(s) and state precisely WHY they do not cleanly apply). (2) MULTI-ARCHETYPE FLAG: does the vault match more than one archetype (e.g. a synthetic-dollar-type vault that is basis/funding-trade AND passive-carry AND holds tokenized CLOs)? If so, NAME them, and record that the required-document set and the risk fields are the UNION of those archetypes' sets — cross-ref knowledge/framework/archetype/critical-documents.md for the document union, and the applicable F-STR-001..030 strategy fields for the risk-field union — not one set. (3) NOVELTY FLAG: is there no-clean-fit on one or more CORE dimensions (Exposure / Strategy / Structure)? If so the vault is a novel/unseen structure: flag that expected-documents.md + risk-field routing may be incomplete for it (see F-ARC-003 for whether the framework has SEEDED it), and that novelty is itself a diligence signal (an untested structure). Show the closest-fit reasoning with an as-of-date, like every other field. This is a self-assessment of CLASSIFICATION COMPLETENESS that mirrors evidence-state E(P): a no-clean-fit flag means "classified as best possible, here is the honest residual", NEVER "did not classify" — fit-acknowledgment supplements a committed classification, it never replaces the effort.
**Data Type**: Structured (per-dimension fit status {clean-single-value | multi-value-set [values listed] | no-clean-fit [closest values + why not]}; multi-archetype flag {archetypes named + doc-set/risk-field union}; novelty flag {core-dimension no-clean-fit -> routing may be incomplete}; closest-fit reasoning; as-of-date)
**Vault Types**: ALL — every vault is classified and its fit residual recorded (a clean-fit vault records clean-single-value across dimensions; a composite / novel vault records the residual). No N/A: fit is always recordable once classification is attempted, since every vault classifies to at least a closest-fit.
**Collection Tier**: T2
**Pillar(s)**: Archetype-level (propose; sibling to F-ARC-001 — the classification-completeness supplement to the archetype tuple; operator confirms)
**Primary Source**: The vault's own disclosed structure / strategy / backing (the same disclosures that drive the dimension classification in dimensions.md), together with the classifier's committed per-dimension values
**Fallback Source**: Reconstruction of the archetype from on-chain / attested holdings and the operator's methodology where a dimension is not directly disclosed; peer-archetype comparison for the closest-fit reasoning
**Evidence Pathway**: Self-assessed against the committed classification: having assigned every dimension a best-effort value, record which dimensions are clean-single-value, which are multi-value-set, and which are no-clean-fit, with the closest-fit reasoning and an as-of-date. The multi-archetype and novelty flags are DERIVED from that per-dimension record, not asserted independently of it.
**Institutional Standard**: The classifier commits to a full best-effort archetype classification and THEN records, honestly, where the vault is composite (multi-archetype) or novel (no-clean-fit on a core dimension) — so the allocator sees both the committed tuple and its honest residual, and the document / risk-field routing pulls the UNION of the matched archetypes' sets rather than silently forcing one. FIT is a factual statement of classification completeness and routing applicability, NEVER a score, grade, rank, or verdict on the vault: a clean-fit vault is not "better" than a novel one — novelty flags only that the framework's routing may not fully cover the vault (a signal about the framework's coverage and the vault's testedness), not a mark against it.
**Status**: Gap with action
**If Not Found — Gap Action**: Full classification committed and per-dimension fit recorded with an as-of-date: E. Classification committed but a dimension's fit-reasoning is thin or the as-of-date is stale: E(P). A dimension genuinely no-clean-fit, with the closest values and the reason they do not apply stated, is a RECORDED fit status (E for the fit assessment), not a gap — and it FEEDS the document audit (novel -> routing may be incomplete; check F-ARC-003 for whether the framework seeded it). The classifier must NOT leave a dimension unclassified to avoid the effort: an unclassified dimension is a process failure, not a no-clean-fit. N/A does not apply — every vault is classifiable to at least a closest-fit.
**Source / Precedent**: an internal analysis (the archetype-classification discussion); knowledge/framework/archetype/dimensions.md (the dimensions the fit is assessed against — "a clean tuple is not a clean vault", and the multi-value / composite exposure-strategy sets); knowledge/framework/archetype/critical-documents.md + expected-documents.md (the document union a multi-archetype vault pulls, and the routing that novelty may leave incomplete). The E(P) mirror is .claude/rules/investigation-core.md / evidence-standards.md (E(P) = tried for primary, it was gated, here is secondary — never "did not try"). a synthetic-dollar vault is the worked multi-archetype case (basis/funding-trade + passive-carry + composes:[a tokenized CLO fund, BUIDL], per dimensions.md and an internal analysis).
**Cross-references**: F-ARC-001 (Strategy-Composition Shift — the sibling archetype-level field; a multi-strategy mix that SHIFTS over time vs this field's fit-at-a-point); F-ARC-003 (Framework Coverage — whether the matched archetype is seeded; the framework-gap counterpart to this vault-fit field: F-ARC-002 says what the vault IS, F-ARC-003 says whether the framework has ROUTING for it); F-DOC-001 (the per-vault document matrix that consumes the fit to know which required-sets apply — the UNION for a multi-archetype vault); the F-STR-001..030 strategy cluster (the risk fields a multi-strategy vault pulls the union of). The `composes:` downward pointer (dimensions.md) records the tokenized assets a multi-archetype vault holds; this field records the fit across the vault's own dimensions.
**Criterion ID(s)**: propose archetype-level (operator assigns); the classification-completeness supplement to the archetype tuple
