# F-STD-005

**Field ID**: F-STD-005
**Category**: Party Standing
**Sub-Category**: Financial Strength
**Field Name**: Party Financial Strength
**What to Collect / Question to Answer**: For each yield-critical party, what financial-strength evidence is available from its public filings — revenue, capital, solvency indicators, assets under management, insurance / E&O cover? Record each figure with its source and as-of-date. Cross-reference F-ENT-023 (manager AUM) and F-CUS-005 (prime-broker insurance), which hold fragments; this field records the party's financial standing as a dated whole rather than a single facet.
**Data Type**: Structured (party -> figure -> source -> as-of-date)
**Vault Types**: ALL (assessed only for parties flagged yield-critical in F-STD-001; N/A for parties not so flagged)
**Collection Tier**: T2
**Pillar(s)**: P18
**Primary Source**: Party public filings — annual report / 10-K / Form ADV / regulatory financial return
**Fallback Source**: Party investor-relations disclosure; the F-ENT-023 (AUM) and F-CUS-005 (insurance) fragments (cross-reference)
**Evidence Pathway**: Third-party-evidenced: obtain the party's public filings and record the financial-strength figures with each source and date.
**Institutional Standard**: The party's financial strength is recorded from its public filings with each figure sourced and dated; fragments held in other fields are cross-referenced, not duplicated.
**Status**: Gap with action
**If Not Found — Gap Action**: Financial-strength figures recorded and dated from filings: E. Partial figures, or dated beyond the threshold for this field type: E(P). Filings exist but are private/restricted: G2. Party publishes no financial evidence and holds no rating: G3 — a finding (solvency cannot be assessed; name the specific filing required). Party not flagged yield-critical: N/A.
**Source / Precedent**: an internal analysis (2026-07-08): party financial strength was captured only in fragments (F-ENT-023 AUM only; F-CUS-004 / F-CUS-005 prime-broker only), never as a dated whole for the yield-critical party.
**Criterion ID(s)**: 18.5 (P18 Party Standing)
