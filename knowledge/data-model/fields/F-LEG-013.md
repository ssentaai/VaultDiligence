**Sub-Category**: Eligibility
**Field Name**: Allocator Eligibility — Legal and Tax Eligibility to Hold
**What to Collect / Question to Answer**: Is THIS allocator (family office / endowment / pension) legally eligible to hold this specific vault token? ERISA eligibility? Accounting classification? Reporting obligations?

**Vault Types**: ALL

**Fallback Source**: Operator offering documents — eligibility criteria stated (accredited investor, qualified purchaser, etc.)
