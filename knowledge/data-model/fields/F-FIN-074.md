# F-FIN-074

**Field ID**: F-FIN-074
**Category**: Financial
**Sub-Category**: Yield Character
**Field Name**: Cash Reserve Coverage for Discretionary Yield Obligations
**What to Collect / Question to Answer**: For discretionary yield sources: what is the issuer's cash reserve coverage? Months of coverage at current distribution rate without additional capital raises. a preferred-equity-backed vault: ~$2.25B reserve, ~28.5 months coverage at 11.5% on ~$3.84B notional.
