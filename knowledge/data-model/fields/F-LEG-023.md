# F-LEG-023

**Field ID**: F-LEG-023
**Category**: Legal
**Sub-Category**: Suspension & Gating Authority
**Field Name**: Discretionary Suspension / Gate / Compulsory-Redemption — IM-vs-PPM Gap
**What to Collect / Question to Answer**: Do the constitutive documents grant the operator or directors discretionary authority to suspend redemptions, impose gates, or compel redemption, and does the marketed Information Memorandum disclose every such power that the binding PPM / Articles actually grant? Is there a gap between what the IM presents and what the PPM permits?
**Data Type**: Text
**Vault Types**: Exposure in {private-credit,structured-credit} OR Management=delegated-offchain-IM OR (Exposure in {equities,corporate-fixed-income,sovereign-fixed-income} AND venue=tradfi-primary)  (derived from VT-N; original "Vault Types" value: VT-3, VT-3a, VT-8)
**Collection Tier**: T3
**Pillar(s)**: P10
**Primary Source**: Binding PPM / Articles of Association — suspension, gating, side-pocket, and compulsory-redemption powers, read against the marketed Information Memorandum
**Fallback Source**: Subscription agreement and directors' resolution authority; operator disclosure of historical use of any suspension or gate
**Evidence Pathway**: Third-party-evidenced: obtain both the IM and the binding PPM/Articles and compare clause-by-clause to confirm every discretionary suspension, gate, or compulsory-redemption power in the binding document is disclosed in the marketed document.
**Institutional Standard**: Every discretionary power to suspend, gate, or compel redemption that the binding PPM or Articles grant is fully and accurately disclosed in the marketed Information Memorandum, with the conditions for invoking each power stated, leaving no IM-versus-PPM gap.
**Status**: Gap with action
**If Not Found — Gap Action**: Obtain the binding PPM/Articles, not only the marketing IM, and list every suspension/gate/compulsory-redemption power; any power present in the PPM but absent from the IM is a critical condition for the allocator. G2 if the PPM is withheld; request it as a precondition.
**Source / Precedent**: Third Point / Och-Ziff and 2008-era hedge-fund gating: funds invoked PPM-granted suspension and gate powers that retail-facing summaries had understated, locking redemptions when liquidity was most needed. The framework requires comparing the marketed Information Memorandum against the binding PPM/Articles to expose any undisclosed discretionary suspension or compulsory-redemption power.
**Criterion ID(s)**: 10.1
