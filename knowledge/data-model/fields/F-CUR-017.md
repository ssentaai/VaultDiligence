# F-CUR-017

**Field ID**: F-CUR-017
**Category**: Curator
**Sub-Category**: Risk Control Operator Diligence Apparatus
**Field Name**: Risk Control Operator Diligence Apparatus — Structured DDQ Process
**What to Collect / Question to Answer**: Does the risk control operator run a structured, documented due-diligence process before a protocol or asset enters its approved universe: one dossier per protocol/asset, numbered review cycles (initial / follow-up / material-change / re-review), defined evidence standards and accepted formats, and explicit decision gates (approve / conditional / reject / defer)? Confirm the process exists as artifacts, not as a claim.
**Data Type**: Structured (dossier presence, review-cycle record, evidence-standard doc, decision-state enum)
**Vault Types**: ALL (curator-managed; N/A for unmanaged/immutable vaults with no active Risk Control Operator — gate off F-ROL-001 Risk Control Operator, do not assume the curator fills it, state why)
**Collection Tier**: T2
**Pillar(s)**: P14
**Primary Source**: Risk Control Operator DDQ manual / documented diligence procedure; sample dossier with review-cycle and decision-state records
**Fallback Source**: Risk Control Operator DDQ response describing the process; screenshots of the dossier/review-cycle system
**Evidence Pathway**: Third-party-evidenced: obtain the risk control operator's documented DDQ procedure and at least one real dossier evidencing per-candidate dossiers, numbered review cycles, evidence standards, and explicit decision states.
**Institutional Standard**: The risk control operator operates a documented DDQ apparatus with per-candidate dossiers, numbered review cycles, stated evidence standards, and named decision states; a candidate is a review object before it can be deployed to.
**Status**: Gap with action
**If Not Found — Gap Action**: Ask the risk control operator for its DDQ procedure and a sample dossier. If onboarding is informal with no dossier/review-cycle/decision-gate record: G3 — the risk control operator must establish and document the apparatus. If documented but untested in production: E(P).
**Source / Precedent**: Concept surfaced by the risk-infrastructure provider methodology (pp.5-7, DDQ intake and vetting); validated on first principles. No risk-infrastructure provider self-reported facts imported.
**Criterion ID(s)**: 14.1
