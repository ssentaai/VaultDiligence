# F-LEG-025

**Field ID**: F-LEG-025
**Category**: Legal
**Sub-Category**: DAO Liability Classification
**Field Name**: DAO Liability Classification — General-Partnership Exposure
**What to Collect / Question to Answer**: Is the governing DAO wrapped in a recognised limited-liability legal entity, or is it an unregistered DAO that a court could classify as a general partnership — exposing governance-token holders, voters, and backers to joint and several liability for the DAO's obligations?
**Data Type**: Text
**Vault Types**: ALL
**Collection Tier**: T3
**Pillar(s)**: P10
**Primary Source**: Company / DAO registry filing evidencing a limited-liability wrapper (e.g. Wyoming DAO LLC, Marshall Islands DAO LLC, foundation) tied to the governing entity
**Fallback Source**: Governance documentation / constitution and on-chain governance-token distribution read to identify whether voting participants are exposed in the absence of a wrapper
**Evidence Pathway**: Third-party-evidenced: confirm the limited-liability wrapper in the relevant company/DAO registry; absent a registry entry, inspect the governance contract and token distribution to characterise who would bear unincorporated-association liability.
**Institutional Standard**: The governing DAO operates through a recognised limited-liability legal wrapper named in a public registry, so governance participants are not exposed to general-partnership joint-and-several liability; an unregistered DAO with no wrapper is disclosed as carrying that exposure.
**Status**: Gap with action
**If Not Found — Gap Action**: Confirm the limited-liability wrapper in the relevant registry; if none exists, state that the DAO may be classified as a general partnership exposing token holders and voters to joint and several liability — a critical condition (RF47). Cure path: adopt a Wyoming or Marshall Islands DAO LLC or foundation wrapper. G3 if no wrapper exists.
**Source / Precedent**: Ooki DAO (N.D. Cal. 2023) and Sarcuni v. bZx DAO (S.D. Cal. 2023): courts treated an unregistered DAO as a general partnership, with governance-token holders and voters held jointly and severally liable (Cal. Corp. Code §16202(a)). F-PRJ-002 captured entity type/wrapper but not this partnership-liability consequence.
**Criterion ID(s)**: 10.1, RF47
