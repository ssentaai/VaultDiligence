# F-LIQ-043

**Field ID**: F-LIQ-043
**Category**: Liquidity
**Sub-Category**: Redemption Process
**Field Name**: Redemption SLA — Normal vs Stressed
**What to Collect / Question to Answer**: What is the operator's stated redemption response time under normal conditions versus stressed market conditions, and is the commitment a formal SLA or an informal expectation?
**Data Type**: Structured (normal T+N, stressed T+N, formal/informal flag, source document)
**Vault Types**: ALL
**Collection Tier**: T2a
**Pillar(s)**: P9
**Primary Source**: Subscription agreement / fund terms / protocol redemption documentation stating the SLA
**Fallback Source**: Operator disclosure distinguishing normal-condition and stressed-condition response times and whether either is contractually binding
**Evidence Pathway**: Inspection-validatable: read the redemption SLA from the fund terms or protocol documentation and record the normal-versus-stressed split and whether it is contractually binding; the divergence between the two is what stress mapping exists to surface.
**Institutional Standard**: A redemption response time is stated for both normal and stressed conditions, the divergence between them is explicit, and the document records whether the commitment is a formal SLA or an informal expectation. Redemption within seven days with partial redemptions permitted and no gating clause is the documented normal-condition baseline.
**Status**: Gap with action
**If Not Found — Gap Action**: Record the stated normal-condition and stressed-condition redemption response times separately and flag whether each is a formal SLA or informal. If only a normal-condition figure exists, state that no stressed-condition commitment is documented. If neither is documented, classify G3 and require the operator to publish the SLA.
**Source / Precedent**: Steakhouse Financial stablecoin-issuer DDQ v1.0 (§6.3): 'response time under normal vs. stressed market conditions? Are there formal or informal SLAs...'
**Criterion ID(s)**: 9.3, RF11
