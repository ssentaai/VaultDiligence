# F-STD-006

**Field ID**: F-STD-006
**Category**: Party Standing
**Sub-Category**: Track Record
**Field Name**: Delegated-Manager Track Record
**What to Collect / Question to Answer**: For the party managing the yield strategy, how long has it managed this strategy, and what is its performance and AUM history in it? Record strategy-specific tenure and performance history with an as-of-date. This is DISTINCT from F-ENT-024 (entity age) and F-ENT-025 (generic stress-event experience), which are proxies scoped to a different party or to generic stress rather than the delegated strategy.
**Data Type**: Structured (party -> strategy -> tenure -> performance/AUM history -> as-of-date)
**Vault Types**: ALL (assessed only for parties flagged yield-critical in F-STD-001; N/A for parties not so flagged)
**Collection Tier**: T2
**Pillar(s)**: P18
**Primary Source**: Party strategy documentation / track-record disclosure / performance history for the specific strategy
**Fallback Source**: Regulatory filing (Form ADV brochure) describing the strategy and tenure; independent performance databases
**Evidence Pathway**: Third-party-evidenced: obtain the party's strategy-specific track record and record tenure and performance with the date; distinguish it from the entity-age and generic-stress proxies (F-ENT-024 / F-ENT-025).
**Institutional Standard**: The delegated manager's tenure and performance on this strategy are recorded and dated from strategy documentation or performance disclosure, not proxied by corporate age or generic experience.
**Status**: Gap with action
**If Not Found — Gap Action**: Strategy-specific tenure and performance recorded and dated: E. Partial, or proxy-only via entity age (F-ENT-024): E(P) — note it is a proxy. Track record exists but is undisclosed: G2. Delegated manager has no disclosed track record on the specific strategy: G3 — a finding. Party not flagged yield-critical: N/A.
**Source / Precedent**: an internal analysis (2026-07-08): F-ENT-025 targets the top-level investment manager and generic stress, not the delegated sub-advisor's strategy-specific record.
**Criterion ID(s)**: 18.6 (P18 Party Standing)
