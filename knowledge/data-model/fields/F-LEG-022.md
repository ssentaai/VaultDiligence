# F-LEG-022

**Field ID**: F-LEG-022
**Category**: Legal
**Sub-Category**: Terms Change Management
**Field Name**: Legal-Terms Change Management + Exit Rights
**What to Collect / Question to Answer**: How can the legal terms governing the vault be amended, who holds the amendment authority, what notice is given to holders, and do holders have any opt-out or exit (redemption) right upon a material amendment?
**Data Type**: Text
**Vault Types**: ALL
**Collection Tier**: T3
**Pillar(s)**: P10
**Primary Source**: Master agreement / PPM amendment clause — amendment authority, notice period, materiality definition, and any opt-out or exit right on material change
**Fallback Source**: Operator-published terms read for the amendment and notice mechanism; on-chain governance/parameter contract read to see whether terms can be changed without a holder-facing delay
**Evidence Pathway**: Inspection-validatable: read the amendment clause and confirm whether the on-chain governance or parameter authority that implements term changes carries a holder-facing notice or timelock that preserves an exit window before changes take effect.
**Institutional Standard**: Amendment authority is named, material amendments require advance notice to holders, and holders hold a documented opt-out or exit right (redemption ahead of effect) on any material adverse term change, so the right to exit on adverse changes is preserved.
**Status**: Gap with action
**If Not Found — Gap Action**: Request the amendment clause and confirm the notice period, the materiality test, and whether an exit/redemption window precedes the effective date. Absence of an exit-on-material-amendment right is a G2 gap (request the clause) or G3 (operator must adopt one); flag if terms are unilaterally amendable without notice.
**Source / Precedent**: Steakhouse §5.9 framework requirement: 'How can your legal terms... be amended? Who has the authority...? do investors have any opt-out or exit rights upon material amendments?' The right to exit on adverse term changes is a core protection and was a tree gap.
**Criterion ID(s)**: 10.1
