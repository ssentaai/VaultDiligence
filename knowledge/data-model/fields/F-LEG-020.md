# F-LEG-020

**Field ID**: F-LEG-020
**Category**: Legal
**Sub-Category**: Liability Caps & Recourse
**Field Name**: Liability Caps / Exclusions / Affiliate Recourse
**What to Collect / Question to Answer**: What legal recourse do holders have against the operator and its affiliates beyond the economic waterfall, and are there contractual limitations of liability, caps, or exclusions — particularly exclusions for 'smart contract risk' — that would hollow out the recoverable claim?
**Data Type**: Text
**Vault Types**: ALL
**Collection Tier**: T3
**Pillar(s)**: P1
**Primary Source**: Master agreement / subscription documents — limitation-of-liability, indemnity, and exclusion clauses, plus the recourse provisions naming operator and affiliate entities
**Fallback Source**: Operator-published terms of service read for liability caps and 'as-is' / smart-contract-risk exclusions; entity registry to confirm which affiliate is the recourse counterparty
**Evidence Pathway**: Third-party-evidenced: obtain the executed agreement and read the limitation-of-liability and exclusion clauses verbatim; confirm the named recourse entity exists and is solvent via a company registry inspection.
**Institutional Standard**: Recourse extends to a named, substantive operator or affiliate entity, and liability caps or exclusions do not exclude the core failure modes (notably smart-contract risk) in a way that would hollow out the claim; the surviving recoverable claim is identified after all exclusions are applied.
**Status**: Gap with action
**If Not Found — Gap Action**: Request the full limitation-of-liability and exclusions section and identify, after exclusions, what claim survives and against which solvent entity. F-LEG-011 confirms a principal-claim enforceability opinion but caps/exclusions/affiliate recourse were uncaptured; a 'smart contract risk' exclusion that voids the claim is a critical condition. G2 if terms exist but are unpublished.
**Source / Precedent**: MF Global (2011): segregation failure left customer claims subordinated and recourse uneconomic; Lehman Brothers (2008): inter-affiliate guarantee and limitation structures determined which creditors recovered. Steakhouse §5.5 framework requirement: 'Are there contractual limitations of liability, caps, or exclusions...?' — liability exclusions for smart-contract risk can hollow out the entire claim.
**Criterion ID(s)**: 1.3
