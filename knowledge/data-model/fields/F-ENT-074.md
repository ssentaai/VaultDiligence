# F-ENT-074

**Field ID**: F-ENT-074
**Category**: Entity
**Sub-Category**: Operator Continuity
**Field Name**: Burn Rate + Implied Runway Months
**What to Collect / Question to Answer**: What is the operator's current monthly cash burn rate and the implied financial runway in months at that burn?
**Data Type**: Currency per month + Number (months)
**Vault Types**: ALL
**Collection Tier**: T3
**Pillar(s)**: P1
**Primary Source**: Operator financial statements / management accounts; direct DDQ response stating burn rate and cash-on-hand
**Fallback Source**: Fundraise disclosures, treasury wallet balances reconciled to disclosed operating cost, investor reporting
**Evidence Pathway**: Third-party-evidenced: obtain the operator's stated monthly burn and cash position from management accounts or DDQ response, and where a treasury is on-chain, cross-check disclosed cash against the inspectable wallet balance to derive runway = cash divided by monthly burn.
**Institutional Standard**: Good practice is a disclosed monthly burn rate and a cash position giving a stated runway of many months (commonly twelve or more), with the figure corroborated by management accounts or an inspectable treasury; a refusal to disclose burn, or a runway of only a few months, is an operator-insolvency continuity exposure independent of contract risk.
**Status**: Gap with action
**If Not Found — Gap Action**: Ask the operator for current monthly burn rate and cash-on-hand, and compute implied runway months. If disclosed verbally only: mark UNVERIFIED and request management accounts. If the operator declines to disclose: G2 — name the operator, state that burn and runway are required to assess mid-allocation insolvency risk.
**Source / Precedent**: Steakhouse Financial stablecoin-issuer DDQ v1.0, section 3.2: 'What is the current burn rate and the implied financial runway?' — operator insolvency mid-allocation is a continuity risk independent of contract risk; the Drift April 2026 episode showed key-person and continuity exposure can crystallise faster than contract risk.
**Criterion ID(s)**: 1.4
